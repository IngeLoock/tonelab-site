What is ToneLAB

Not a gym. A collective of studios that take training seriously.

ToneLAB is a Newcastle-owned fitness brand offering three distinct ways to train: Reformer Pilates, Performance strength and conditioning, and Training built on HIIT and Interval Weight Training.

Every studio, session and coach is driven by one belief: everyone deserves purposeful, high-quality training, whatever their background, ability or stage of life. Welcoming, focused and community-led.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can I train at more than one studio?

Yes. An All Access membership works at every ToneLAB studio across all three disciplines. Discipline-specific memberships cover every studio of that type.

I've never done Reformer Pilates. Where do I start?

ToneCONTROL and ToneREFORMER are the best first classes. Every session is instructor-led and scaled to your pace, so you don't need any experience.

How do I book a class?

Download the ToneLAB app, choose your studio and book straight from the timetable. You can also join a waitlist for full classes.

Is there a lock-in contract?

Intro offers have no lock-in. Membership terms vary by plan and are shown clearly before you sign up.

Where are the studios?

Nine studios across Newcastle (The Junction, Lambton, Hamilton North, Newcastle West, Wallsend), Lake Macquarie (Warners Bay, Toronto) and Port Stephens (Raymond Terrace).
